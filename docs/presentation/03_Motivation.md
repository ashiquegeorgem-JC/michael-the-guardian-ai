# Slide 03: Motivation & Research Vision

## Bridging Raw Telemetry to Unified Threat Context

---

### Key Points
- **Shift from Reactive to Proactive:** Move beyond post-incident forensics to near-real-time intrusion detection.
- **Explainable Machine Learning:** Replace opaque scalar scores with mathematically decomposable, transparent contributing signals.
- **Forensic Evidence Provenance:** Preserve unbroken, immutable links from high-level threat indicators down to the exact source log line numbers.
- **Sovereign Human Oversight:** Automate evidence discovery and correlation without stripping the human analyst of triage authority.

### Presenter Notes
"Our motivation was to build an intelligent force multiplier for security analysts. We wanted an architecture that detects intrusions autonomously, correlates related events into campaign stories, and provides explainable evidence without making destructive automated decisions."
